## TL;DR

Writing is how the artifacts become authority. Long-form, deep, practical — written for India GCC leaders, AI architects, FinOps practitioners and governance professionals. Cadence: one article per month, 1,200–1,800 words, with a reusable template and a sanitized artifact attached.

:::note
Articles are written to be **reusable**: each one becomes a talk, a LinkedIn carousel and a template. Write once, distribute many.
:::

## Why writing matters for this seat

- GCC hiring for senior architect seats is referral- and reputation-driven; cold applications rarely win.
- A public body of work is proof-of-competence a resume cannot convey.
- Thought leadership positions you as a peer to the CIO/CTO/CDO — critical for "high strategic visibility" roles.
- It compounds: each artifact becomes content; each content piece becomes a referral touchpoint.

## Content pillars (rotate; never post off-topic)

1. **AI governance as code** — turning NIST AI RMF / ISO 42001 / EU AI Act / DPDP / RBI rules into enforceable platform controls and evidence.
2. **Regulated-enterprise AI** — BFSI and healthcare/pharma AI use cases, risk tiering, model governance, audit readiness.
3. **AI FinOps** — unit economics (cost per inference/token/outcome), GPU strategy, chargeback, cost-aware architecture.
4. **India regulatory landscape** — DPDP, RBI FREE-AI/draft framework, SEBI circulars, India AI Governance Guidelines; what they mean for builders.
5. **Transformation playbooks** — operating models, CoE design, decision frameworks, build-vs-buy, vendor evaluation.

## Article template

```markdown
---
title: "Article Title"
description: "One-sentence summary (≤160 chars)."
tags: [writing, <pillar>]
---

## The problem (2–3 paragraphs)
What breaks today, in business terms.

## The frame (1 short section)
The concept or model being introduced.

## The mechanism (the bulk)
Concrete controls, patterns, code/config snippets, tables.

## A worked example
One real-ish scenario end to end.

## What to do Monday
3–5 actionable steps.

## Further reading
Links to site pages and external sources.
```

## Distribution

- **LinkedIn:** 2–3 posts/week (short insight + one carousel/doc per month).
- **LinkedIn Articles / Medium:** 1 long-form/month (1,200–1,800 words).
- **GitHub:** sanitized artifacts attached to articles.
- **Talks/webinars:** 1/quarter at India AI/GCC forums.
- **Podcasts:** 1–2/quarter as guest.
- **Communities:** weekly thoughtful commenting in India GCC, FinOps Foundation, AI governance groups.

## Guardrails

- Lead every post with business outcome / transformation; governance and FinOps are the *how*.
- Never post pure compliance citations; always translate (control + evidence + metric).
- Mix in architecture and FinOps content so the brand reads "transformation architect," not "auditor."
- Keep it constructive and forward-looking; no criticism of employers.
