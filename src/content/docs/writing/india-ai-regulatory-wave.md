## TL;DR

India's AI regulatory landscape is being written *now*: DPDP Act (deadline May 2027), RBI draft AI framework + FREE-AI, SEBI May 2026 circular (10,000+ entities), IRDAI guidelines, India AI Governance Guidelines (Feb 2026). For architects, the opportunity is to build the control set before the rules finalize — and to position as the person who can map regulation to platform controls. Base draft — to be expanded.

## The problem

Most organizations are waiting for "final rules" before acting. By then, the control set, the evidence and the audit trail will be built by someone else — and that someone will be the architect who moved first. The window is now: drafts already define the control set; build to the draft and adjust.

## The frame

India is a **pro-innovation jurisdiction with light-touch horizontal rules and deepening sectoral regulation**. The horizontal layer (DPDP, India AI Governance Guidelines) sets tone; the sectoral regulators (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, TRAI, ICMR, NHA) set obligations. See [India overview](/thought-base/india/) for the full stack.

## The mechanism

### DPDP Act (horizontal data)
Consent, purpose limitation, data principal rights, breach notification, SDF obligations. Deadline May 2027. Controls: consent ledger, purpose tags, rights API, erasure cascade, SDF readiness. See [DPDP](/thought-base/governance/dpdp/).

### RBI (banking)
Draft AI framework + FREE-AI (7 principles, 26 recommendations): board-approved AI policy, model risk management, vendor oversight, fairness audits, incident reporting (24hr). See [RBI/SEBI/IRDAI](/thought-base/governance/india-regulators/).

### SEBI (securities)
May 2026 circular: AI adoption and threat modeling for 10,000+ financial entities. Controls: threat model per system, security review, documentation, monitoring.

### IRDAI (insurance)
Cyber/AI guidelines: AI register, cyber controls, monitoring, third-party oversight.

### India AI Governance Guidelines (Feb 2026)
Sectoral regulators expected to issue AI-specific rules. Watch the sectoral layer, not just the horizontal document.
