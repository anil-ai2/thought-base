## TL;DR

AI architects in India (2026): advertised 19–46 LPA (median ~34), senior/GCC/product roles 50–80+ LPA, top earners (staff/principal at global GCCs) 1 Cr+. GCCs pay 12–60% above IT services. Comp is driven by scope (global vs. regional), budget authority, teams enabled, direct reports and whether AI is a P&L or cost center.

## Bands (India, 2026)

| Segment | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AI architect (advertised, all) | 19–46 LPA (median ~34) | Aggregated listings; wide variance |
| Senior / GCC / product AI architect | 50–80+ LPA | GCCs and product MNCs |
| Top earners (staff/principal, global GCCs) | 1 Cr+ | Strategic scope, global mandate |
| IT services (TCS/Infosys/Wipro) | Caps lower; heavy operational delivery | Avoid as primary target |
| GCC premium over IT services | 12–60% | For identical roles |

## Comp drivers (what moves you up the band)

1. **Scope:** global vs. regional mandate. Global = higher.
2. **Budget authority:** do you own a budget line, or only advise?
3. **Teams enabled:** how many engineers/teams consume your standards?
4. **Direct reports:** even dotted-line reports increase comp.
5. **P&L vs. cost center:** AI as a P&L driver pays more than AI as a cost center.
6. **Vertical:** regulated verticals (BFSI, pharma) pay premium for compliance depth.
7. **Employer type:** GCC > product MNC > consulting > IT services.
8. **Location:** Bengaluru/Mumbai premium; Hyderabad/Pune/Chennai slightly lower.
9. **Credentials + artifacts:** ISO 42001, FinOps CP, a public control library and reference arch repo convert into comp leverage.

## How to position for the top of the band

- **Lead with transformation outcomes**, not tools: "defined standards for X, governed Y controls, enabled Z teams, reduced risk/cost by $."
- **Negotiate scope explicitly:** architecture & governance ownership, platform team runs ops, no on-call, budget/standards authority, reporting line to CIO/CTO/CDO.
- **Bring artifacts:** a control framework, risk tiering model and reference architecture repo are proof-of-competence a resume cannot convey.
- **Target GCCs of regulated global enterprises** — the deepest premium pool.
- **Build visibility:** referrals dominate senior GCC hiring; a credible brand converts into inbound invites and negotiation leverage.

## Common pitfalls

- **Optimizing for title over scope.** A "Chief AI Architect" with no budget authority pays less than a "Principal" with global scope.
- **Accepting operational ownership.** On-call and delivery responsibility cap strategic comp and consume the time you need for standards.
- **Undervaluing the trifecta.** Law + tech + FinOps is rare; price it as a premium, not a discount.
- **Ignoring the GCC premium.** Same role, 12–60% more — employer type is a comp lever.
- **Not negotiating.** Senior architect comp is almost always negotiable; the first offer is a floor.
