## TL;DR

India's GCCs are where in-house strategic AI architect seats live: 1,800–2,100 centers, ~1.9–2.1M professionals, $64–98B in value, paying 12–60% above IT services. Target the GCCs of global regulated enterprises (BFSI, pharma) in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai and Mumbai. Avoid domestic IT services as primary employers.

## Employer types (ranked for this target)

1. **GCCs of global regulated enterprises (BFSI, pharma)** — best fit: strategic seats, premium comp, platform teams run ops, you govern.
2. **Product MNCs with India R&D centers** — high comp, fewer seats, often more hands-on.
3. **Global consulting / Big-4 internal AI teams** — high comp, strategic scope, but more delivery pressure.
4. **Domestic IT services (TCS/Infosys/Wipro)** — avoid as primary: comp caps, operational-delivery push, less strategic scope.
5. **Indian product startups** — variable; some strategic seats, but comp and stability vary widely.

## How to find and target seats

- **Job boards:** LinkedIn, Naukri — search the title strings in [About](/about/) / [India overview](/india/).
- **Filter:** India, GCC / MNC / product company, BFSI or Healthcare/Pharma, senior/staff/principal level.
- **Network:** India GCC forums, NASSCOM, cloud user groups, FinOps Foundation India chapter.
- **Informational interviews:** target current Chief/Head AI Architects at 5 GCCs; learn hiring bar, refine positioning, build referrals.
- **Referrals dominate:** GCC senior seats are referral-driven; cold applications rarely win. Branding and community presence convert into referrals.
