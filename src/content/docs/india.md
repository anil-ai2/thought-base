## TL;DR

India is a **pro-innovation jurisdiction with light-touch horizontal rules and deepening sectoral regulation**. The horizontal layer (DPDP Act, India AI Governance Guidelines) sets tone; the sectoral regulators (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, TRAI, ICMR, NHA) set obligations. For an architect, the opportunity is that these rules are being written *now* — early movers shape the standard and command premium compensation.

## The regulatory stack

| Layer | Instrument | Status | Architect's action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horizontal (data) | DPDP Act 2023 | Compliance deadline May 2027 | Build consent ledger, purpose tags, rights API, SDF readiness |
| Horizontal (AI) | India AI Governance Guidelines (Feb 2026) | Issued; sectoral rules to follow | Watch sectoral regulators; align to principles |
| Sectoral (banking) | RBI draft AI framework + FREE-AI | Draft released | Register, model lifecycle gates, fairness, vendor assessment |
| Sectoral (securities) | SEBI May 2026 circular | Issued (10,000+ entities) | Threat modeling, security review, documentation |
| Sectoral (insurance) | IRDAI cyber/AI guidelines | Issued | AI register, cyber controls, monitoring |
| Sectoral (telecom) | TRAI (expected) | Pending | Watch |
| Sectoral (health) | ICMR / NHA / CDSCO | Emerging | Watch; healthcare AI growing fast |

## The GCC opportunity

India hosts **1,800–2,100 Global Capability Centers (GCCs)** employing ~1.9–2.1M professionals, generating $64–98B in value. GCCs pay **12–60% more than IT services** for identical roles and are where in-house strategic architect seats actually live. Domestic IT services (TCS/Infosys/Wipro) cap comp and push operational delivery.

**Target employers:** GCCs of global BFSI and pharma enterprises in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Mumbai.

## Target verticals

| Tier | Vertical | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **BFSI** | Strongest regulatory density (RBI/SEBI/IRDAI + DPDP); highest budgets; AI in fraud/risk/underwriting/claims; comp ceiling highest |
| 1 | **Healthcare & Pharma** | 155 GCCs, ~320K professionals, ~67K jobs added in 2026; AI in drug discovery, clinical trials, diagnostics; talent scarcity premium |
| 2 | **Telecom** | Highest agentic AI adoption (48%); TRAI rules emerging; fewer architect seats, more infra |
| 3 (avoid as primary) | Retail/CPG, manufacturing, energy | Good AI adoption but less regulation → lower differentiation and comp ceiling |
