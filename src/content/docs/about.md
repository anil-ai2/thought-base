## Positioning statement

> Enterprise AI Transformation Architect for regulated enterprises — I translate law, regulation and risk into platform controls, reference architectures and FinOps models that make AI safe, compliant and cost-efficient at scale. (BFSI + Healthcare/Pharma, India GCCs.)

This statement is used verbatim across LinkedIn headline, resume summary, email signature and talk bios.

## The target seat

- **Archetype:** "Architect of the system of systems." You own AI strategy, transformation roadmap, governance operating model, reference architectures, control framework, FinOps model, standards, review boards, vendor evaluation and executive reporting.
- **You do NOT own:** on-call, pipelines, clusters, incident response, ticket triage, data-engineering delivery. Those live with a platform/AI engineering team you influence but do not operate.
- **Leverage:** standards + guardrails + enablement. Make 100 engineers compliant and cost-efficient without touching their code.
- **Negotiated explicitly at offer stage:** "I set the architecture and governance; a platform team runs it." If the JD includes on-call or "hands-on delivery," it is the wrong seat.

## Target titles

Chief AI Architect · Head of AI Architecture · VP AI Transformation · Principal AI Architect · Distinguished Engineer (AI) · AI Governance Architect · AI Platform Architect.

## Target employers

GCCs of global regulated enterprises in India (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Mumbai). Primary verticals: **BFSI** and **Healthcare/Pharma**. Secondary: Telecom. Avoid domestic IT services as primary employers (comp caps, operational-delivery push).

## Meta-vertical

**"Regulated / complex enterprises"** rather than a single industry or pure generalist. This class values the law + tech + FinOps trifecta maximally, pays premium, gives strategic visibility (regulator-facing work = board attention) and minimizes operational overhead (platform teams run; you govern).

## The trifecta

### 1. Law & Compliance
Law degree. Working frameworks: NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act risk classes, GDPR data sovereignty, DPDP Act, RBI/SEBI/IRDAI rules, India AI Governance Guidelines. Translated into enforceable platform controls with automated evidence — never cited as article numbers in executive settings.

### 2. Technology & Architecture
20 years cloud and operations. Competent in CI/CD, Kubernetes, data engineering, application delivery, databases. Review-level depth (challenge designs, spot anti-patterns, estimate cost) rather than implementer depth. Building toward LLMOps/GenAI ops, RAG, vector stores, evals, guardrails, AI security and AI observability.

### 3. FinOps
FinOps Foundation framework, with leadership and finance personas primary and engineering as a co-primary executor. Focus on AI unit economics: cost per inference, per token, per outcome; GPU utilization; training vs. inference split; commitment strategy; chargeback/showback.

### Amplifier — founder experience
10 years as founder. Frames every architecture decision against value delivered and P&L impact. "I've owned P&L and built 0→1, so I architect for value, not novelty."
