## TL;DR

Reusable templates you can copy and adapt: control library, risk tiering intake, exec-line answer bank, top failure modes checklist, ADR template and worked examples. Each is a starting point, not a finished product — adapt to your context.

## How to use

1. Copy the template into your own repo/wiki.
2. Adapt columns and rows to your context (regulations, systems, owners).
3. Version it; review quarterly.
4. Publish a sanitized slice as thought leadership (see [Writing](/thought-base/writing/)).
