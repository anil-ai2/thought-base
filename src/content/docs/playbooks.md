## TL;DR

Playbooks are the **reusable, copy-pasteable operating system** for enterprise AI transformation: an operating model with gates, a CoE charter, decision frameworks (build-vs-buy, model selection, vendor scorecards) and the risk tiering model. They are what turns a single architect into a force multiplier across 100 engineers.

:::tip
Start with the [Operating Model](/playbooks/operating-model/) — it shows where every other artifact fits.
:::

## Why playbooks matter for this seat

A strategic architect does not ship code; they ship **standards, gates and enablement**. Playbooks are the vehicle. They encode the decisions so that teams self-serve within guardrails, and the architect's time goes to the genuinely novel problems. A good playbook reduces review load by 80% and time-to-production by weeks.

## Operating principles for playbooks

1. **Each playbook has an entry and exit criterion.** No "vibes-based" progression.
2. **Each has a decision owner.** RACI is explicit; "everyone is responsible" means no one is.
3. **Each has a template.** A playbook without a template is advice.
4. **Each has a sunset clause.** Revisit annually or when a framework (NIST, ISO, EU AI Act delegated acts) changes materially.
5. **Each is versioned.** v1, v1.1, … with a short "changed because" note.
